# Privacy Policy for Daily Focus — Tasks & Habits

**Effective Date:** October 13, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

## Overview
**Daily Focus — Tasks & Habits** ("Daily Focus", "we", "our", or "the app") is developed as an offline-first productivity tool. Your privacy is paramount. This application is engineered so that your personal tasks, timer history, and preferences never leave your device.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Daily Focus does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or device data.

- **Tasks and Habit Entries:** Any titles, descriptions, categories, completion timestamps, or tags you create are stored exclusively in your device's local application storage (using Android SharedPreferences).
- **Timer and Focus Data:** Focus sessions and countdown configurations operate purely within your local app runtime.
- **Preferences:** App settings (such as light or dark theme choices) are persisted strictly on your local device.

## 2. Network Access and Permissions
Daily Focus operates entirely offline:
- The app does not request or require the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission.
- The app does not connect to external servers, cloud storage, APIs, or databases.
- The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, device storage outside the app sandbox, or hardware identifiers.

## 3. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not embed telemetry or crash-reporting services (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Mixpanel).
- **No Advertising:** Daily Focus contains zero advertisements, ad SDKs, or ad trackers.
- **No User Accounts:** There is no registration, login, social sign-in, or backend account synchronization.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
All data created in Daily Focus is under your complete control:
- You can edit or delete individual tasks at any time from the app's task list.
- You can clear all tasks, timer states, and settings at once via **Settings > Clear All Local Data**.
- Uninstalling the application immediately deletes all local data stored by the app on your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Daily Focus does not knowingly collect any data from children of any age. The app is safe for general audiences as no data is collected from any user.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions, the revised policy will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via your designated support email address.
